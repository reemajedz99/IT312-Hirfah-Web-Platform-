Hirfah Club

Learn a craft, make something with your hands, and meet people who love it too.

Hirfah is a community for handmade crafts. We run workshops in pottery, embroidery, crochet, painting and traditional Saudi crafts for complete beginners and for makers who want to improve their skills.
